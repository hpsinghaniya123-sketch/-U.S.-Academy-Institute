Welcome to U.S ACADEMY & INSTITUTE

U.S ACADEMY & INSTITUTE is a CBSE Board school located in Rajpur, Campierganj, Gorakhpur. Our school provides students with a positive, safe and disciplined environment for learning.

Our main focus is on study and academic development. We encourage students to concentrate on their education, develop good learning habits and work towards achieving their goals.

Along with education, we also believe in discipline, good character, responsibility and respectful behaviour. Our aim is to help students become confident and responsible individuals.
