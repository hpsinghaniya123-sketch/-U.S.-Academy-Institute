Our School

U.S ACADEMY & INSTITUTE is a CBSE Board school located in Rajpur, Campierganj, Gorakhpur. Our school provides students with a supportive, safe and disciplined environment for learning.

Our main focus is on studies. We encourage students to concentrate on their education, attend classes regularly and develop strong learning habits.

We believe that education is not only about books and examinations. Students should also develop confidence, responsibility, discipline and good character.
